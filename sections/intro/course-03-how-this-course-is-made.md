# How This Course Is Made

Authors map sections to the official Certified GitOps Associate (CGOA) objectives and check mechanisms against primary documentation. They review examples for responsible components, prerequisites and meaningful failure cases. No command output is invented.

The labs and playgrounds are checked statically, which means their configuration, scripts and documents are validated, and they have not yet been run end to end. Structural checks verify the manifest, reading order, section introductions, closing summaries and quiz files. Technical review remains necessary before publication.

The [contributors](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS067/graphs/contributors) maintain the material. To [report a mistake](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS067/issues), identify the page and disputed statement. The [license](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS067/blob/main/LICENSE) governs reuse.

Objectives belong to their respective owners. Teaching and knowledge checks are original practice material. Application names are illustrative and have no special technical meaning.
